I'm a Master's student in Informatics with a Bachelor's in Information Security.

I spend most of my time focused on web security and reconnaissance. I really enjoy the mapping phase figuring out how an application is built, how its pieces connect, and where the logical blind spots are.

I've always been good at troubleshooting and diagnostics. I find that understanding how things fail under normal conditions is usually the best way to figure out how they can be exploited.

Outside of hands-on work, I read a lot of security research papers. I follow conference talks closely because I like tracking how the theoretical vulnerabilities discussed in academia actually show up in real-world web applications.
